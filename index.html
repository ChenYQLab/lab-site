ABOUT THE LAB

从免疫机制解析到广谱疫苗设计

实验室聚焦流感病毒、新冠病毒等呼吸道病毒的感染免疫与疫苗研发，重点研究病毒变异背景下的B细胞免疫应答、抗体反应及BCR免疫组库，解析广谱保护性免疫形成的关键机制与保守抗原靶点。

我们结合单细胞测序、BCR免疫组库、高通量人源单克隆抗体筛选、结构生物信息学与AI辅助蛋白设计等技术，围绕流感病毒HA/NA、新冠病毒关键抗原等开展理性免疫原设计，推进广谱流感疫苗、黏膜疫苗及治疗性人单克隆抗体的研发与临床转化。

从免疫机制中寻找答案，以理性设计推动更广谱、更持久的免疫保护。

RESEARCH

我们的研究

聚焦呼吸道病毒广谱防护，围绕疫苗、抗体与B细胞免疫开展四个相互支撑的研究方向。

01

广谱流感疫苗

Broadly Protective Influenza Vaccines

聚焦流感病毒HA与NA等关键抗原，解析保守保护性表位与广谱抗体应答机制，并结合反向疫苗学、结构预测与免疫原设计，开发可应对不同亚型及变异株的广谱流感疫苗。

  • HA / NA
  • 保守表位
  • 反向疫苗学
02

广谱新冠黏膜疫苗

Broadly Protective Mucosal Vaccines against SARS-CoV-2

针对新冠病毒持续变异及现有疫苗阻断传播能力有限的问题，开展广谱免疫原与黏膜疫苗设计，探索鼻腔免疫诱导IgA及系统性免疫应答的机制，提升对变异株的交叉保护与传播阻断能力。

  • RBD免疫原
  • 黏膜免疫
  • IgA
03

治疗性人源单克隆抗体

Therapeutic Human Monoclonal Antibodies

建立人源单克隆抗体筛选与功能评价体系，从病毒感染和免疫应答中发现具有广谱活性的保护性抗体，解析其抗原表位与作用机制，并推动候选抗体向治疗和临床转化。

  • 人源单抗
  • 高通量筛选
  • 临床转化
04

B细胞免疫应答与BCR免疫组库

B-cell Immune Responses & BCR Repertoires

利用单细胞测序与BCR免疫组库分析，解析病毒感染和疫苗接种后B细胞克隆扩增、胚系基因使用与抗体应答的动态变化，揭示广谱保护性免疫形成规律，并为疫苗理性设计提供依据。

  • 单细胞测序
  • BCR组库
  • 克隆演化

ABOUT THE LAB

From Immune Mechanisms to Broadly Protective Vaccine Design

Our laboratory focuses on immunity to respiratory viral infections, including influenza and SARS-CoV-2, and the development of next-generation vaccines. We investigate B-cell immune responses, antibody immunity, and BCR repertoires in the context of viral evolution, with the goal of identifying conserved antigenic targets and the mechanisms underlying broad and durable immune protection.

By integrating single-cell sequencing, BCR repertoire profiling, high-throughput human monoclonal antibody screening, structural bioinformatics, and AI-assisted protein design, we pursue rational immunogen design targeting key viral antigens such as influenza HA and NA. Our research aims to advance broadly protective influenza vaccines, mucosal vaccines against SARS-CoV-2, and therapeutic human monoclonal antibodies from discovery toward clinical translation.

We uncover the mechanisms of immunity and translate them into rational designs for broader and more durable protection.

RESEARCH

Our research

We pursue four interconnected research directions spanning vaccines, antibodies, and B-cell immunity to achieve broad protection against respiratory viruses.

01

Broadly Protective Influenza Vaccines

广谱流感疫苗

We investigate conserved protective epitopes and broadly reactive antibody responses to key influenza antigens, including HA and NA, and integrate reverse vaccinology, structural prediction, and immunogen design to develop vaccines with broad protection across influenza subtypes and variants.

  • HA / NA
  • Conserved Epitopes
  • Reverse Vaccinology
02

Broadly Protective Mucosal Vaccines against SARS-CoV-2

广谱新冠黏膜疫苗

We develop broadly protective immunogens and mucosal vaccine strategies against SARS-CoV-2, with a focus on intranasal immunization, IgA-mediated mucosal immunity, and systemic responses that enhance cross-variant protection and reduce viral transmission.

  • RBD Immunogens
  • Mucosal Immunity
  • IgA
03

Therapeutic Human Monoclonal Antibodies

治疗性人源单克隆抗体

We identify and characterize broadly protective human monoclonal antibodies through high-throughput screening and functional assays, define their antigenic epitopes and mechanisms of action, and advance promising candidates toward therapeutic development and clinical translation.

  • Human mAbs
  • High-throughput Screening
  • Clinical Translation
04

B-cell Immune Responses & BCR Repertoires

B细胞免疫应答与BCR免疫组库

Using single-cell sequencing and BCR repertoire profiling, we characterize B-cell clonal expansion, germline gene usage, and antibody responses following viral infection and vaccination, providing mechanistic insights to guide the rational design of broadly protective vaccines.

  • Single-cell Sequencing
  • BCR Repertoire
  • Clonal Evolution
