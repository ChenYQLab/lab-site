ABOUT THE LAB

从机制解析到理性设计

实验室围绕病毒感染、宿主免疫和疫苗保护的关键问题开展研究。我们关注免疫细胞如何识别抗原、克隆如何扩增与迁移，以及免疫记忆如何在不同组织和时间尺度上形成。

通过将单细胞测序、TCR/BCR组库、空间成像、结构生物信息学和生成式蛋白设计相结合，我们构建可检验的机制假设，并以有限而高质量的湿实验完成验证。

数据告诉我们发生了什么，机制解释为什么，而设计让我们决定下一步发生什么。

ABOUT THE LAB

From mechanism to rational design

Our laboratory studies key questions in viral infection, host immunity, and vaccine-mediated protection. We investigate how immune cells recognize antigens, how clones expand and migrate, and how immune memory develops across tissues and over time.

By combining single-cell sequencing, TCR/BCR repertoire profiling, spatial imaging, structural bioinformatics, and generative protein design, we formulate testable mechanistic hypotheses and validate them through focused, high-quality experiments.

Data show us what happened. Mechanisms explain why. Design lets us shape what happens next.

RESEARCH

Our research

Four interconnected areas organized along one translational path.

01

Infection & Tissue Immunity

感染与组织免疫

We map immune-cell states, clonal expansion, and migration across tissues after viral infection to distinguish protective from pathological responses.

  • Single-cell RNA-seq
  • Spatial imaging
  • Longitudinal cohorts
02

Adaptive Immune Repertoire

免疫受体组库

We integrate single-cell and bulk TCR/BCR data to study diversity, clonal sharing, lineage evolution, and antigen specificity.

  • scTCR/BCR-seq
  • AIRR
  • Clonotype modeling
03

Broad-spectrum Vaccine Design

广谱疫苗设计

For influenza and emerging viruses, we combine sequence evolution, structural constraints, and immune data to prioritize broadly protective antigen combinations.

  • Antigen evolution
  • DMS
  • Multivalent display
04

AI Protein & Antibody Design

AI蛋白与抗体设计

We use structure prediction, generative models, and multi-objective optimization to design protein nanocarriers, antibodies, and binders in an experimental screening loop.

  • Generative design
  • Structure prediction
  • Multi-objective optimization
